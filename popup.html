Show Nukes in Airspace ☢ Atom Bombs 💣 Hydro Bombs 🚀 MIRV Adds a UI panel that shows all nukes currently in the airspace (global counts). ▶
Player Stats Overlay ⚔ Troops 💰 Gold Adds rows to the player-info overlay (shown when you hover a player): their natural troop growth from the game's formula, and their gold income measured from their gold changes over the last 60 s — same model as your own panels, including a base/ports/warships/factories breakdown. ▶
Trade Partner 💰 Trade ⚔ Naval Adds rows to the player-info overlay (shown when you hover a player). Income shows the gold you receive from trading with them, per second and per minute — port-to-port trade ships, captured ships, and train stops at each other's cities/ports/factories. Naval rows split each event into "theirs" (your navy vs them) and "mine" (their navy vs you): trade-ship captures, transport ships destroyed, and warships destroyed. ▶
Gold Income Display 💰 Gold Shows your gold income per second and/or per minute. The total is measured from your gold deltas, then split into base vs ports vs factories: the passive base is read from Config, and trade-ship arrivals (ports) / train station stops (factories) are detected client-side and valued with the exact Config payouts. Conquest loot from killing players is never counted. Draggable panel, position is remembered. ▶
Troop Rate Display ⚔ Troops Shows your troop growth per second and/or per minute. Uses the exact game formula, so it matches the server even while you're under attack. Draggable panel, position is remembered. ▶

Settings are stored locally. You won't need to redo them.
